Federal ASC regulations, with notes on what changed

John J. Goehle presents 42 CFR Part 416 in regulatory order, with annotations explaining recent CMS changes.

  • Medicare Conditions for Coverage for ambulatory surgery centers
  • ASC payment provisions and changes through the CY 2027 final rule
  • ASC Quality Reporting Program provisions

This book focuses on the regulation itself. The Survey Guide for ASCs is a separate reference that also covers CMS Interpretive Guidelines and survey procedures.
