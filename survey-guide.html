2026 Edition · Fully revised & expanded

Be ready the day the surveyor walks in

CMS certification, recertification, and deemed-status surveys arrive unannounced — on an ordinary operating day, with no warning. The centers that pass are the ones already running to the standard every single day. This fully revised 2026 Edition turns the federal regulations into a working desk reference you can actually use: marked up, tabbed, and survey-ready.

Every requirement appears with the exact rule, the exact guidance, the exact survey procedure, and a plain-language Author's Note explaining what it means, how centers get cited, and what to have documented. It's the difference between a binder you assemble before a survey and a culture you build so the survey is just a normal day.

Be Compliant · Be Prepared · Be Confident · Be Ready · Be Safe

Inside the 2026 Edition

  • Every Condition for Coverage with the regulation, CMS interpretive guideline, survey procedures, and a plain-language Author's Note
  • All current Appendix I Q-tags, fully cross-referenced — and the most-cited tags, with exactly how to fix each one
  • Accreditation & deemed status: all five CMS-approved accrediting organizations
  • Infection control, QAPI, patient rights, emergency preparedness & Life Safety
  • New: condition-by-condition operational dashboards
  • New: a 30/60/90-day readiness plan & annual compliance calendar
  • New: survey-day playbooks & ready-to-use evidence templates
  • A Plan of Correction that actually holds, plus a 75-question self-assessment quiz, checklists & three navigation indexes

Regulatory content current to the CMS State Operations Manual (Appendix L) and 42 CFR Part 416 as verified in 2026. ISBN 979-8-18457-593-3 (paperback).
