Questions about the books

Who writes these ASC books?

John Goehle, MBA, CASC — a veteran ambulatory surgery center administrator and advisor with three decades in the field, and host of The ASC Podcast.

What is The Survey Guide for ASCs?

A working desk reference that turns the CMS Conditions for Coverage and Interpretive Guidelines into something you can actually use on survey day — every requirement paired with the exact rule, the survey procedure, and a plain-language explanation. The 2026 Edition is fully revised and expanded.

What is Ambulatory Surgery Center Governance about?

A practical guide for ASC owners and governing-body members to the responsibilities they assume and the CMS regulations they must answer to. The Second Edition serves as a companion to The Survey Guide.

Are the books available now?

The 2020 Survey Guide and the first-edition Governance book are available on Amazon today. The fully revised 2026 Survey Guide and Second-Edition Governance are releasing soon — join the notify list to hear first.
